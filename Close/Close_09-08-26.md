In crypto, both Bitcoin (-1.55%) and Ethereum (-0.98%) followed risk assets lower on the day, though both retain solid weekly and monthly gains, suggesting the pullback is likely a consolidation rather than a trend reversal.

## 3. Risks

- **Elevated crude oil and a strong dollar create a stagflationary squeeze:** WTI at $94.06 (+20% MoM) raises input costs across consumer goods, transportation, and manufacturing, while a DXY at 118.07 squeezes import-dependent economies and compresses dollar-denominated revenues for U.S. multinationals. Together, these forces could pressure corporate earnings guidance revisions lower in Q3 reporting season and reduce the Federal Reserve's room to cut rates, potentially trapping markets in a higher-for-longer rate environment precisely when growth is showing signs of softening.
- **Consumer Discretionary weakness signals demand fragility:** XLY was today's worst-performing sector (-1.33%), is down -1.96% on the week, -2.70% on the month, and has essentially flatlined over 3 and 6 months (+0.04% and +0.41% respectively). As the highest-cost consumer environment in years — driven by elevated energy prices, a strong dollar lifting import prices, and rates still above 3.5% — discretionary spending faces structural headwinds. A sustained breakdown in XLY could be an early warning of broader economic softening that would ultimately weigh on all risk assets.
