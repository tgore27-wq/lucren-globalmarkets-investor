## 1. Market Summary

With the DXY elevated at 120.33 and credit spreads under pressure — HYG down 0.42% and LQD down 0.72% in tandem — the risk-off tone appears broad-based rather than sector-specific, leaving the market in a fragile technical posture as it closes out September.

If credit spreads widen meaningfully from here — particularly in high yield — risk assets across equities and crypto could face accelerated selling pressure irrespective of sector-specific fundamentals.
