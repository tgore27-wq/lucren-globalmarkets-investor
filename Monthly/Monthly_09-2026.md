## 1. Market Summary

The month closes with the equity market standing on a narrow and increasingly precarious foundation: the path into Q4 will be determined by whether the bond market's higher-for-longer verdict is confirmed by incoming inflation data, or whether a durable easing in energy prices and Fed rhetoric allows breadth to recover and the rally to broaden.
