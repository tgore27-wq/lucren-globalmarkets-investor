## 3. Risks

- **10-year yield approaching 5.00%:** At 4.96%, the 10-year Treasury is within 4 basis points of the psychologically and technically significant 5% level. A breach would almost certainly intensify selling in rate-sensitive sectors (Utilities -1.92%, Real Estate -1.55% already leading declines), compress equity multiples further, and could trigger algorithmic stop-loss selling across asset classes — representing the single largest near-term technical risk in the market.
- **Breadth deterioration signals underlying market fragility:** With only 28.8% of S&P 500 names above their 50-day MA, 49.1% above their 200-day MA, and 30 new 52-week lows forming against only 11 new highs, the index-level headline numbers (+1.59% weekly for S&P 500) are masking significant internal deterioration. This divergence between index performance and constituent health is a classic late-cycle warning sign — any further macro disappointment could accelerate a broader unwind.
- **DXY at 119.51 compounds multi-asset pressure:** The U.S. dollar at highly elevated levels continues to act as a global liquidity tightener, suppressing commodities (gold -1.73%, silver -4.58% today), pressuring emerging market assets (Hang Seng -0.29%, Shanghai -1.22% overnight), and creating significant headwinds for S&P 500 multinational earnings translations — a risk that may not be fully priced into current equity valuations.
