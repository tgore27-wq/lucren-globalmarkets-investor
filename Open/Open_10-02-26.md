## 3. Risks

- **NFP miss triggers recession-repricing:** Today's Non-Farm Payrolls forecast (89K) is already well below the prior print (162K), and any downside surprise could sharply reprice growth expectations, triggering a broad risk-off move. The equity market is currently positioned with muted near-term vol (VIX9D 14.00), meaning a weak print could create a rapid and disorderly repricing of both equities and the Fed rate path — particularly damaging for the rate-sensitive sectors (Real Estate, Utilities, Financials) that are already underperforming.
- **Structural internal weakness beneath headline resilience:** Despite the S&P 500 trading near highs (7,666), only 23.1% of constituents are above their 50-day MA and new 52-week lows outnumber new highs four-to-one. This breadth divergence historically precedes broader market corrections when the narrow leadership group (Technology) falters. Any catalyst — macro disappointment, policy shift, or sector-specific shock — that rotates out of mega-cap tech could expose the underlying weakness and amplify downside across the broader index.
- **Elevated 10-Year yield and strong DXY compressing equity risk premium:** With the 10-Year Treasury at 5.29% and the DXY at an elevated 120.33, the risk-free rate continues to compete aggressively with equity valuations. High rates are already visible in the underperformance of Real Estate (XLRE -7.63% monthly), Utilities (XLU -6.77% monthly), and Consumer Discretionary (XLY -5.04% monthly). If the long end of the curve continues to sell off — particularly if today's NFP comes in hotter than expected — the compression of the equity risk premium could trigger a broader de-rating across growth and rate-sensitive assets simultaneously.
