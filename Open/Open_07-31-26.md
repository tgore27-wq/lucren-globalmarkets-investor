## 3. Risks

- **Month-end rebalancing distortion:** The magnitude of today's opening gains — particularly the KOSPI +17.91% and the Nasdaq +2.78% — carries the hallmarks of mechanical month-end rebalancing rather than a durable fundamental catalyst. Investors should be cautious about treating these levels as a new baseline; once rebalancing flows exhaust themselves mid-morning, a reversal or gap-fill toward prior closing levels is a plausible intraday risk.
- **DXY at 120.71 is a persistent macro headwind:** A dollar at historically elevated levels creates ongoing headwinds for U.S. multinational earnings, commodity-importing sectors, and emerging-market capital flows. Should today's ECI or UoM data come in hotter than expected, the dollar could extend further, weighing on gold, metals, and any equity rally dependent on loose financial conditions — particularly pressuring the Nasdaq's heavily international revenue base.
- **Sector divergence signals incomplete breadth:** Healthcare (-1.64%), Real Estate (-1.44%), and Utilities (-0.56%) are all declining at the open while the headline indices post large gains. This split between growth-driven leadership and defensive underperformance, combined with an unavailable NYSE A/D ratio, raises the risk that today's rally is narrow and index-cap-weight-driven rather than broadly confirmed — a condition that has historically preceded short-term reversals when the leading sector (here, Technology) loses its intraday momentum.
