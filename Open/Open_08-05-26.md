## 3. Risks

- **ADP Employment miss amplifying rate-cut uncertainty:** The ADP forecast of 68K versus a prior 98K already implies a meaningful labor market slowdown. A print materially below 68K could rattle equity markets by raising stagflation concerns — weak growth without the Fed having sufficient room to cut quickly given 4.63% long yields and a still-elevated DXY.
- **Technology overextension and potential for sharp mean-reversion:** XLK's +9.24% weekly gain and Nasdaq's +7.86% WTD surge have moved both instruments far above short-term fair value. Today's -0.83% Nasdaq open is an early warning. If profit-taking accelerates — particularly ahead of the President's speech at 4:30 PM ET — tech could drag the broader S&P 500 meaningfully below the 7,720 support level, reversing a significant portion of this week's gains.
- **Crude oil weakness signaling global demand deterioration:** WTI's -11.25% weekly plunge and -26.70% three-month collapse is an unusually sharp contraction that may not be purely supply-driven. Sustained crude weakness historically precedes downward earnings revisions in energy, materials, and industrials, and can signal weakening global growth expectations — a macro risk that has not yet been fully priced into equity multiples at current elevated S&P 500 levels above 7,700.
