Extreme single-day moves in crypto have historically preceded sharp reversals that can spill over into risk sentiment more broadly — particularly for retail-heavy segments of the equity market. If crypto reverses violently intraday, it could add an additional layer of negative sentiment to an equity market already opening lower across all major indices.
