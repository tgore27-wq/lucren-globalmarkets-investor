If the catalyst is domestic (credit event, policy shock, or geopolitical escalation on the Korean peninsula), the risk of contagion into broader EM and Asian-Pacific assets warrants close monitoring. A widening of EM credit spreads would be an early warning signal.
- **Precious metals in structural downtrend — deflationary or liquidity signal:** Gold (-24.71% over six months) and Silver (-31.05% over six months) are in steep, prolonged declines despite elevated global uncertainty. Combined with a very strong DXY (120.71), this pattern may reflect dollar liquidity stress or forced deleveraging in commodities broadly. If selling pressure in metals intensifies, it could signal tightening financial conditions that are not yet fully reflected in equity volatility measures.
