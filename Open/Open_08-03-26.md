With the 10:00 AM ISM Manufacturing PMI print (forecast: 54.0) serving as the primary macro catalyst of the day, the tone is cautiously bullish — index-level strength is clear, but sector dispersion, an elevated DXY at 120.71, and the crude sell-off introduce enough cross-current risk to temper outright conviction.

## 3. Risks

- **WTI Crude Oil -6.27% is a significant macro warning signal:** A single-session decline of this magnitude in crude — even as equities rally — may reflect a downward revision to global demand growth or an unexpected supply development; if this move proves fundamental rather than technical, it could pressure Energy sector earnings and eventually feed through to broader risk sentiment.
- **Elevated DXY at 120.71 is a persistent headwind:** The dollar index at historically high levels compresses the earnings of U.S. multinationals, particularly in Technology (XLK -5.53% monthly), and continues to weigh on commodities including gold (-21.14% six-month) and silver (-28.85% six-month); any further dollar strength could cap the current equity rally's durability.
- **Tech sector divergence undermines index-level strength:** With XLK trading -0.22% at the open despite the S&P 500 up +1.31% and the Nasdaq up +2.05%, the apparent Nasdaq gains are likely concentrated in a narrow set of names rather than broad-based; a market where the Technology ETF lags materially on an up day is a sign of underlying sector rotation rather than true breadth expansion, and breadth confirmation remains essential to validate this move.
